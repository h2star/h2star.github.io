Privacy Policy

Xiaoli (小麗) — last updated 31 August 2026

1. Who this covers

Xiaoli is a private personal assistant operated by and for a single individual, its owner. It has no other users and accepts no sign-ups.

2. What Google data is accessed

Xiaoli requests these Google OAuth scopes, and no others:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events — to read, create, and update events on the owner's own calendars.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.calendarlist.readonly — to read the list of calendars the owner subscribes to, so events are filed under the right calendar.

Xiaoli does not request or access Gmail, Google Drive, Google Contacts, or any other Google service.

3. How the data is used

Calendar data is used solely to answer the owner's own questions and to carry out the owner's own instructions — for example, listing the day's schedule or adding a meeting the owner asked for. It is not used for any other purpose.

4. Storage

Xiaoli runs on the owner's personal computer. OAuth credentials are stored in the macOS Keychain on that computer. Calendar content is not copied into any database or long-term store; it is fetched when needed to answer a question and then discarded.

5. Sharing

Google user data is never sold, rented, published, or transferred to any third party. It is never used for advertising, profiling, or analytics, and it is never used to train any machine-learning model.

6. Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke Xiaoli's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access immediately and permanently ends all data access. Stored OAuth credentials can be deleted from the macOS Keychain at any time.

7. Limited Use

Xiaoli's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: h2star@gmail.com

Xiaoli is a personal, non-commercial project with a single user.
